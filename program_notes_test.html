Program Notes - Style Variations

Style 1: Subtle (With +/−)

Very discreet, minimal styling. Light italic text with simple +/− indicator.

Piano Trio No. 2 in E♭ Major, D. 929

Franz Schubert

(1797-1828)

Program Notes

Composed in 1827, near the end of Schubert's short life, the Piano Trio No. 2 is a large-scale, deeply expressive chamber work that blends lyricism, emotional depth, and classical balance.

Spanning four movements, the trio moves from a grand and expansive opening Allegro, through a poignant second movement (Andante con moto)—featuring one of Schubert's most memorable melodies—followed by a graceful Scherzo and a spirited, rhythmically charged Finale.

Style 2: Bordered Card

More prominent with subtle border and background. Arrow indicator rotates when open.
