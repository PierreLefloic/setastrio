Franz Schubert - Piano Trio No. 2 in E-flat major

Movement II: Andante con moto

Movement IV: Allegro moderato

Composed in 1827, near the end of Schubert’s short life, the Piano Trio No. 2 is a large-scale, deeply expressive chamber work that blends lyricism, emotional depth, and classical balance. Spanning four movements, the trio moves from a grand and expansive opening Allegro, through a poignant second movement (Andante con moto)—featuring one of Schubert’s most memorable melodies—followed by a graceful Scherzo and a spirited, rhythmically charged Finale. The work demonstrates Schubert’s gift for long, singing lines and harmonic richness, while subtly foreshadowing the Romantic era. It remains one of the most beloved and frequently performed works in the piano trio repertoire.

Joan Tower - Trio Cavany

Trio Cavany is a dynamic and rhythmically driven work for piano trio (violin, cello, and piano) composed by American composer Joan Tower. The title is a portmanteau of the commissioning ensembles—Camelia Piano Trio, Van Swieten Society, and Ny Chamber Music Festival. The piece showcases Tower’s signature energetic style, marked by shifting rhythms, bold gestures, and strong interplay between the instruments. It unfolds in a single movement, with tightly woven textures and vigorous momentum, often emphasizing percussive piano figures and lyrical string lines. Trio Cavany reflects Tower's modern American voice—bold, expressive, and rhythmically alive.
